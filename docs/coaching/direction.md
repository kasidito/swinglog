# Approved design direction and quality bar

This records the user's supplied brief, not a claim that an automated design workshop ran.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** A golfer sees a realistic fictional human pose, reads one short Thai action, then checks an observable result.
- **OWN-WORLD:** A light cream and forest green coaching handbook with a coordinated dark mode. Keep the SwingLog name, private offline operation and free access. Photographic illustrations teach rather than decorate.
- **STORY:** Choose minutes/trays and practice topic → review the plan → open a drill → follow manually selected steps → log flight observations → review history. Related foundations return to the same drill progress.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Show a useful setup illustration, practical entry to its lesson, and time/tray choices. No unsubstantiated performance claims, testimonials or marketing statistics.
- **FORM:** Operate/read on a phone held between shots; clear image, numbered action, self-check, deliberate previous/next controls and image zoom. The supplied brief defines the form. No independent FORM seed was corroborated.

## Quality bar

Six foundations and all 20 legacy drill IDs must be present, each drill has 3–5 image steps. Thai text, target direction and numbers remain outside the raster. Mirror according to actual source handedness and maintain lead/trail meanings. Do not infer a universal body angle or stance distance from an AI image.

Check 360/390/768 px, light/dark, large text, left/right, keyboard and zoom; retain old history, saved steps and PR #12's analysis behavior. A completed online cache must support subsequent offline instruction use. Missing media must leave useful readable guidance.

Agent image screening, browser automation, beginner comprehension and professional accuracy are four different checks. The last two have not occurred and must remain pending. The app does not claim certification or measured launch-monitor values.
